The sachets

A box of predatory mites hangs in the plants and stops working after four weeks. Nothing about it belongs to one plant, so it is not an event on a plant: it is one standing job the collection has, with a run that ends. The app already has a line for exactly this — the one that asks for a backup and stays until you make one.

The sachet carries a printed week number. That number and the day you hung them are the only two things written down, and they are overwritten the next time — there is no history to keep, because nothing would ever be asked of it.

1 · Today, while a run is good

Nothing shouts. One line under the sync status, in the voice the rest of the app uses for a fact: what is hanging, and how far into its four weeks it is. It is the same counting the plant rows do — days, in mono, backwards from something that happened.

2 · Saying that new ones hang

Two things can be true and only two: the week printed on the packet, and the day you hung them. So that is the whole sheet. It opens already filled in with this week and today — the answer nine times out of ten — and one button commits it. The same two fields are the whole of the settings, because there is nothing else about this to configure.

What gets written down

Two fields: { week, hungOn }. Not a log — one pair, overwritten every time you hang new ones, because no question is ever asked of the old one. Four weeks is a constant in the code, not a setting; it is what the packet claims and it is the same for every packet. "Day 23 of 28" is counted from hungOn when the screen draws, so nothing derived is stored and the two devices cannot disagree. No plant code and no place, so nothing here has to be kept in step when a plant moves, dies or leaves.
