Undo bar

not optional

Logging is one tap with no confirmation, so there has to be a way back. It says what it did, in the past tense, with the plant named — you may be two rooms away by the time you read it. It stays about five seconds and never blocks anything.

Bottom sheet

everything WATER isn't

Reachable with a thumb, so it rises from the bottom and the things you press most sit lowest. Water-with-a-choice is expanded at the top because it is the reason the sheet exists; the rest are single taps that log and close.
